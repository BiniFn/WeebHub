Features

Everything you need to enjoy
anime and manga, your way.

WeebHub is purpose-built for anime fans — from smart library scanning to watch parties.

📚

Smart Library Scanner

Seamlessly scans your local files and matches them with AniList. Fast, accurate, and fully automated.

⬇

Automated Downloads

Set up rules to automatically fetch new episodes as soon as they air. Your library updates while you sleep.

▶

Stream from Any Source

Stream episodes from torrents, Debrid services, or online sources with minimal waiting.

📖

Complete Manga Reader

Download and read chapters from your favorite sources. Track progress automatically.

🎉

Watch Parties

Invite friends to watch anime together with WeebHub's built-in watch party feature.

⚡

Transcoding & Direct Play

Leverage FFmpeg transcoding and hardware acceleration for smooth playback on any browser.

🧩

Extension System

Install community extensions to add new sources and features. Or build your own in JavaScript.

🎵

Discord Rich Presence

Show what you're watching on Discord automatically with built-in OAuth account linking.

📱

Watch Anywhere

Access your entire library on the go. The Android app connects to your home server over Wi-Fi.

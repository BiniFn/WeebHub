## Features tailored to the way you consume anime and manga.

### Smart library scanner

WeebHub seamlessly scans your local files and matches them with AniList. It's fast, accurate, and completely automated.

![Smart library scanner](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-31-06-56-36.webp?updatedAt=1761893868577)

### Automated downloads

Set up rules to automatically fetch new episodes from your favorite sources as soon as they air. Your library updates while you sleep.

![Automated downloads](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/guides/img-2026-01-27-10-38-21.webp)

### Stream from various sources

Stream episodes and movies directly from your torrents, Debrid services or online sources with little to no waiting.

![Stream from various sources](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/guides/img-20260507-162208-zcx8acnh--sq.webp)

### Complete manga reader

Download and read chapters from your favorite sources. Track your progress automatically and discover new series right from the app.

![Complete manga reader](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-31-10-27-30.webp?updatedAt=1761906460132)

### Host watch parties

Invite friends to watch anime together with WeebHub's built-in watch party feature. Or, share your local library across multiple instances.

![Host watch parties](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-04-15.webp?updatedAt=1761764667368)

### Transcoding & direct play

Enjoy your local library on any web browser. Leverage FFmpeg transcoding and hardware acceleration for smooth playback anywhere.

![Transcoding & direct play](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-17-38.webp?updatedAt=1761765498237)

### Powerful extension system

Extend WeebHub's capabilities. Install extensions created by the community to add new sources and functionalities to the app. Or, create your own entirely in JavaScript.

![Powerful extension system](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-36-02.webp?updatedAt=1761766573324)

### Feature-rich built-in player

Enjoy a wide range of features to enhance your watching experience. From Anime4K sharpening to real-time subtitle translation.

![Feature-rich built-in player](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-17-13-15.webp?updatedAt=1761758012992)

### Integrated downloading

Extensible search and downloading with built-in support for your favorite BitTorrent clients and Debrid services.

![Integrated downloading](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-17-26-17.webp?updatedAt=1761758807749)

### Binge-watch with ease

Use playlists to quickly queue up episodes and movies. Watch them in order, seamlessly transitioning from one episode to the next.

![Binge-watch with ease](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-17-30-51.webp?updatedAt=1761759097949)

### Discover & track airings

Find new series to watch or read and stay on top of the release calendar. Never miss an episode of your favorite ongoing anime.

![Discover & track airings](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-40-07.webp?updatedAt=1761766822862)

### Watch anywhere

Access your entire anime and manga library on the go. WeebHub's web app works beautifully on mobile devices and supports external players.

![Watch anywhere](https://i.postimg.cc/T1P0LWpc/img-2024-10-26-15-25-57-2x.png)

### True offline mode

Enjoy your downloaded media on the go, without an internet connection. Sync your local changes once you're back online.

![True offline mode](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-32-55.webp?updatedAt=1761766385831)

### Endless customization

Make the app truly yours. Tweak colors, fonts, and the layout to create an interface that perfectly matches your aesthetic preferences.

![Endless customization](https://WeebHub.app/bucket/img-2025-10-29-19-20-16.webp?updatedAt=1761765631677)
